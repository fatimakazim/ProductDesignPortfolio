Overview

Project Link: https://fatimakazim.github.io/FinalProject/public/

Traditional productivity tools often rely on stressful, ticking countdowns that can induce anxiety rather than focus. To solve this, I created Study Until the Ice Melts, an ambient, 3D focus timer where your session lasts exactly as long as the virtual ice takes to melt. Operating as both a UI/UX Designer and Front-End Developer, I owned this project from end to end. I designed the elegant, dark-mode interface in Figma, and engineered the custom 3D physics, glass refraction, and spatial audio engine entirely from scratch using Vanilla JavaScript and Three.js. The result is a seamless Progressive Web App (PWA) that bypasses the app store to deliver a native, calming, and organic time-management experience directly in the browser

Features

  • Ice Melt Mode

    Set temperature (1–5) and cube count (up to 8). Session length is organic — approximate by design, not a fixed countdown.

  • Pomodoro & Deep Work

    25-minute and 60–90 minute fixed modes. The app auto-calculates the correct ice configuration and drops it into the scene.

  • Real-Time 3D Environment

    Glass, water, and physically-simulated ice rendered via Three.js with MeshPhysicalMaterial, clearcoat, refraction, and ACESFilmic tone mapping.

  • Spatial Audio Engine

    Ambient loops (Rain, Café, White Noise) plus randomized ice clink sounds on collision — all routed through the Web Audio API.

  • Progressive Web App

    Installable from the browser. Offline-capable via service worker. Renders edge-to-edge on iOS behind the Dynamic Island.

Technical Highlights

01

Custom Physics — No Library

Gravity, restitution, and per-frame damping written from scratch inside the Three.js render loop. Each cube carries its own velocity state, age counter, and settled flag.

02

Baked Environment Maps

Runtime-generated via WebGLCubeRenderTarget — no external HDRIs, no decode overhead. Per-cube vertex displacement produces organic, non-uniform ice silhouettes.

03

Audio Unlock Architecture

Splash screen captures the first user gesture to initialize AudioContext and prime all tracks at zero volume — cleanly bypassing iOS and Android auto-play restrictions.

04

Mobile Performance

Pixel ratio capped at 2. 180ms throttle on collision audio. Stable framerates across tested iPhones without thermal throttling.

Challenges & Solutions

01

Mobile Audio Auto-play Policies

Problem: iOS and Android block AudioContext creation until a direct user gesture is detected.

Solution: Onboarding splash doubles as an audio unlock gate — the first tap initializes and primes all tracks before the session begins.

02

3D Performance on Mobile

Problem: Refractive MeshPhysicalMaterial is GPU-intensive — drops below 30fps and causes thermal throttling on mobile.

Solution: Baked environment maps at startup eliminate HDRI decode cost. Pixel ratio capped at 2. ~40% GPU load reduction on tested devices.

03

Native Feel Without an App Store

Problem: Browser chrome breaks immersion on iOS — the Safari toolbar signals "webpage," not "environment."

Solution: PWA + service worker + apple-mobile-web-app-status-bar-style="black-translucent" renders the canvas edge-to-edge behind the Dynamic Island.
